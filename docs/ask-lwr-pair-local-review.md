## Required review items

1. **SQL/function design.** One new SECURITY INVOKER helper `ai_live_private.eligibility_pair(uuid,uuid,jsonb)` behind existing server-only `public.ai_live_lookup`. Three phases: resolve both authorized subjects, read the selected division facts, re-read and audit the resulting snapshot. Existing operations retain their implementation. Additive audit JSON and intent allowance only; no eligibility table or backfill.
2. **Exact facts.** Resolution: member reference and display name, at most five choices for one slot; optional validated page-team division. Final private read: two member references/names; each applicable established Season or PrimeTime rating; RF; independently derived source-NR boolean; active same-season division references. Envelope: season/division, rating kind, active Rules version, division min/max/pair configuration and opaque snapshot fingerprint. Raw current DUPR numeric values are not returned. RF, marker, placement list and fingerprint stay server-side.
3. **Commissioner authorization.** Existing resolved Commissioner/League Manager access to active members is reused. Captain/club-pro relationships are independently checked for each subject and again for the requested season. Choosing A never authorizes B.
4. **Ordinary Player behavior.** Denied safely under current boundaries; no partner-fact access expansion. Broader ordinary-player partner eligibility remains a separate owner/security decision.
5. **Name resolution.** Generic Unicode names and SELF, no Terry/Tim constants. Exact unique full names resolve directly. Partial names require explicit choice even if only one current match. Selected references are reauthorized on every turn. Duplicate subjects rejected.
6. **Ambiguity.** Up to five authorized name choices for the unresolved slot. Indistinguishable display labels request a more specific name without revealing contact details. No guessing or general directory listing.
7. **Division clarification.** Uses active catalog and explicit question context or an authorized team-page hint. Asks for exact league/division; larger catalogs narrow by league, season and division number, at most eight options per turn. No PDF fallback for pair intent.
8. **Context retention.** Reuses encrypted, purpose/session-bound expiring Live receipts; retains both subject choices and selected context. Browser-supplied raw member/division IDs are ignored. Forged or cross-session choices fail. Rules replacement invalidates policy context while retaining reauthorized subject choices.
9. **Deterministic calculation.** Integer millionths arithmetic, current official table and verified division configuration, independent status classification, individual checks and combined maximum. Missing/conflicting facts or unsupported policy semantics fail closed. No answer-model calculation.
10. **Rated behavior.** Uses established applicable Season DUPR, including PrimeTime Season DUPR for PrimeTime. Missing Rated value is unknown, not guessed from current DUPR.
11. **NR blank-rating behavior.** NR with blank numerical Season rating is evaluated from the proposed division under Rule 4.5.1. Maximum truncated to a tenth minus the parsed official adjustment; highest applicable adjustment across verified current same-season placements; never reduce an existing assigned numerical NR value. No rating is stored. Unknown RF/NR/placement basis yields cannot-determine.
12. **Combined/team limits.** Adds both applicable values and compares with the official pair maximum; applies ordinary individual bounds to Rated players and the existing NR exception.
